# Addendum No. 1 to Tender Documents

**Project:** Construction of a Two-Storey Community Health Centre  
**Tender Ref:** TSHC-2024-001  
**Date:** 05 August 2024

## 1. Clarification to Clause 4 (Price Escalation)

It is clarified that, notwithstanding the firm-price basis, the Employer shall allow
price variation only for the steel component of the reinforcement work based on the
published steel price index on the first day of the month of tender opening and the
month of supply. No other material or component shall be eligible for escalation.

## 2. Extension of Tender Submission Date

The last date for submission of tenders is extended from 15 September 2024 to
22 September 2024, 15:00 hrs IST.

## 3. Additional Drawing

Drawing No. TSHC-2024-001/ADD-1 showing revised column locations for the ground
floor is added to the tender set. The Contractor shall price the works based on the
revised drawing.

## 4. Deleted Specification Item

Item S-45 (marble flooring in waiting area) is deleted from the Schedule of
Specifications. No substitute is required and the corresponding BOQ item is
withdrawn.
