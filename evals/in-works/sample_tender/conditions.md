NOTICE INVITING TENDER (NIT No. TS/DEMO/2026/001)

Name of work: Construction of a 4-storey office building with one basement,
including foundation, deep excavation adjacent to an existing structure, RCC
superstructure, brick masonry, plaster, waterproofing and allied civil works.
The site has a high sub-soil water table (below ground water table works are
involved). Time allowed for completion: 30 (thirty) months.

[p2]
SPECIAL CONDITIONS OF CONTRACT (SCC)

Clause 14 — Price basis. The contract shall be executed on a firm price basis
and no escalation whatsoever shall be payable to the contractor for the entire
duration of the contract, notwithstanding any variation in the cost of
materials, labour, fuel or statutory levies.

[p3]
Clause 21 — Payment of running account bills. Interim payment against a
running account bill shall be released to the contractor within 120 (one
hundred twenty) days of certification by the Engineer-in-Charge, subject to
availability of funds.

[p4]
Clause 33 — Compensation for delay. If the contractor fails to complete the
works within the stipulated time, liquidated damages at the rate of 1% (one
percent) of the contract value shall be levied for each week of delay.

[p5]
Clause 41 — Defect liability and retention. The defect liability period shall
be 36 (thirty-six) months from the date of completion. Retention money at the
rate of 10% (ten percent) of each running account bill shall be withheld and
released only after expiry of the defect liability period.

[p6]
Clause 52 — Termination. The Employer may terminate the contract for its
convenience at any time by giving 15 days written notice, without assigning any
reason, and in such event the contractor shall have no claim for compensation,
loss of profit or damages of any kind.
