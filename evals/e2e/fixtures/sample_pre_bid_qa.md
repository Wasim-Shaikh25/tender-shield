# Pre-Bid Queries and Replies

**Project:** Construction of a Two-Storey Community Health Centre  
**Tender Ref:** TSHC-2024-001

## Q1: Site access and possession

**Q:** Will the site be handed over in one phase or in parts?  
**A:** The complete site will be handed over in one phase on 01 October 2024.

## Q2: Mobilisation advance

**Q:** Is any mobilisation advance payable and what is the recovery schedule?  
**A:** A mobilisation advance of 10% of the contract price is payable against a bank guarantee. Recovery shall be made by deducting 20% of each running bill until fully recovered.

## Q3: Steel brand and testing

**Q:** Are contractors allowed to use any ISI-marked steel brand or only those listed in the tender documents?  
**A:** Any ISI-marked steel brand equivalent to or superior to the specified grades is acceptable, subject to submission of mill test certificates and third-party test reports.

## Q4: Price escalation

**Q:** Clause 4 states a firm price. Does this apply to steel and cement also?  
**A:** Yes. The contract is on a firm-price basis and no escalation shall be allowed for steel, cement, or any other material.

## Q5: Sub-contracting

**Q:** Is sub-contracting of specialised works such as HVAC and lifts permitted?  
**A:** Sub-contracting is permitted with prior written approval of the Engineer-in-Charge. The Contractor shall remain fully responsible for the acts and defaults of any sub-contractor.

## Q6: Defect liability period

**Q:** Is the 24-month defect liability period negotiable?  
**A:** No. The Defects Liability Period of 24 months is mandatory and non-negotiable.
